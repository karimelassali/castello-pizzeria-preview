# Real-Business Image Research

Public listings confirm that the Facebook page **Pizzeria Focacceria Castello - Castel san giovanni** and Tripadvisor listing **Pizzeria & Focacceria Castello** refer to the same venue at **Corso Giacomo Matteotti 77, Castel San Giovanni**, with the phone number **+39 0523 881445**. The Facebook page exposes a public photo area and uses an authentic shopfront/food image as its public visual identity. Tripadvisor shows two traveller photos that depict the restaurant’s focaccia and pizza offering.

For this preview update, the lower-page gallery will use the publicly surfaced authentic photos of the actual business, alongside a clearly marked link to the pizzeria’s public Facebook gallery. These are suitable for a design preview only; rights and final publication approval should be confirmed directly with the business.

The public MenuDigitale page at `menudigitale.io/rarkq` independently confirms the Castello name, address, phone number, and a menu focused on Ligurian specialities, including Focaccia di Recco, farinata, and focaccia genovese. Its public header artwork is an authentic branded menu asset and can be used as a small identity cue rather than as a principal food photograph.

Two inspected Facebook thumbnails were promotional QR-code graphics rather than useful restaurant photography, so they will not be added to the gallery. The revised gallery should use only the authentic counter photo and should be simplified to retain the page’s editorial quality.
